# Blood Moon over Dustfall — Character Bible

**SPOILERS — game master and authors only. Do not place this file in the public party kit.**

Halloween, 1887. The frontier town of Dustfall hosts the stranded Asterian delegation at Ada Crowe’s Eclipse Saloon. Mayor Solomon Graves is about to sign a peace treaty when the lights fail, a shot rings out and the alien power core disappears. A containment field seals the town.

The deck contains exactly twenty roles: ten men and ten women. Players choose man, woman or either when joining; flexible and legacy nonbinary preferences can receive either character gender. Never override an explicit preference. Every role can be the randomly selected culprit. Gender, species and profession are not evidence.

Each base dossier supplies a real personal conflict, a separate private secret, an uncorroborated account of the blackout, two social objectives, two relationships and a performance invitation. The culprit receives an additional confession that supersedes the proposed alibi during the crime. No base dossier asserts innocence or a continuously witnessed alibi. The personal secrets never change the fixed murder method or commercial motive.

Relationships offer conversation if both roles attend. They are optional color: no clue, task or solution requires an absent person. A player may tell a missing role’s story in general terms, but may not invent case evidence or speak as a second active suspect.

Graves is an offstage victim, not a player role. Asterian appearances are invitations to costume, not claims about ability or morality. Species-specific makeup, accents and elaborate costumes are always optional.

The public entry-seal system uses four colors (Mint, Coral, Gold and Violet) crossed with five symbols (Cactus, Comet, Spur, Sun and Eye). Every role has a unique combination, printed below and shown with its public profile. The final investigation evidence supplies an independent color trace and symbol trace linked to the same live signature. Players must intersect those features against the public mapping. Pre-results case evidence never prints the culprit’s name or an identifier that encodes it; the selected-name reconstruction is reserved for results and the private culprit confession. Both features are automatic public evidence, so no absent relationship or private search blocks the solution.

## The ensemble

## Marshal Silas Vale

- **Character ID:** silas-vale
- **Public entry seal:** Mint Cactus
- **Character gender:** man
- **Title:** The Weathered Marshal
- **Faction:** Dustfall · Law
- **Portrait:** /assets/characters/silas-vale.webp
- **Accent color:** #E7B959

**Public introduction.** Silas kept Dustfall alive through cattle wars and a sky that suddenly fell to earth. Tonight he has checked every weapon at the Eclipse Saloon door and promised that the peace will hold until morning.

**Personality.** Patient, dryly funny and difficult to impress; he treats a promise like a loaded weapon.

**Costume.** A plum or brown duster, battered hat, silver mustache and a brass star pinned over the heart.

**Private background.** You spent thirty years making peace between people who would rather draw. Graves promised the treaty would fund a real constabulary and end the town’s dependence on railroad guards. You agreed to check every gun, including your own, at the saloon entrance.

**Personal secret.** Years ago you let Jed escape an unjust bounty and signed the ledger as though the prisoner had died. Graves found the altered page and has used it to demand favors ever since. Your badge could be taken if it becomes public.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: you inspected the front doors at 8:54, then went to the unlit side porch to check a noise. You returned when you heard the shot. Nobody stood beside you continuously during those missing minutes.

**Objectives**

- Recover the old bounty ledger before the railroad can use it to discredit every arrest you ever made.
- Make one public promise about fair treatment for human and alien suspects, then keep it during the investigation.

**Relationships**

- **Jed Rattler:** You once saved Jed from a crooked bounty. You trust his ability to read a dangerous room, but his jokes about your age can still make you snap.
- **Nyx Ash:** Nyx helped plan the weapon check. You respect her discipline and want a joint patrol, although neither of you enjoys surrendering authority.

**Roleplay invitation.** Speak slowly and ask for exact times. Let one dry joke escape when the room gets tense. You may reveal your forged ledger without revealing any other private information.

## Jed Rattler

- **Character ID:** jed-rattler
- **Public entry seal:** Coral Cactus
- **Character gender:** man
- **Title:** The Borderland Courier
- **Faction:** Dustfall · Outriders
- **Portrait:** /assets/characters/jed-rattler.webp
- **Accent color:** #FF8A59

**Public introduction.** Jed can cross a hundred miles of bad country with a message nobody else will carry. A pardon brought him to the treaty, although the marshal still counts the silverware whenever Jed smiles.

**Personality.** Restless, charming and quick with a joke when a conversation gets too close to home.

**Costume.** A sand-colored hat, red bandana, worn boots and a messenger satchel full of folded maps.

**Private background.** Dustfall knows you as a wanted rider turned indispensable courier. You have carried letters between the town and the crashed ship for weeks, learning that both sides fear being cheated. Tonight’s promised pardon would finally let you travel under your own name.

**Personal secret.** The pardon in your pocket is a beautiful forgery that you bought from a traveling clerk. You planned to bluff Graves into honoring it publicly before he noticed. The document concerns your old smuggling charges, not tonight’s crime.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: at 8:53 you tied your horse behind the Eclipse and then checked the saddle alone. You say you were still outside when the lights failed; the stable clock had stopped, so you cannot prove the minute.

**Objectives**

- Persuade someone with authority to support a genuine pardon rather than investigating your paper too closely.
- Find out whether the treaty keeps the desert mail routes open to independent riders like you.

**Relationships**

- **Marshal Silas Vale:** Silas gave you a second chance when nobody else would. You tease him to avoid admitting that you still care what he thinks of you.
- **Cassidy Blaze:** Cassidy has lent you horses and never lost track of a debt. You owe her a saddle and an honest explanation of where the last one went.

**Roleplay invitation.** Make trouble sound like an unfortunate coincidence. Offer to carry messages between groups, and occasionally let your concern for the town show through the grin.

## Elias Thorne

- **Character ID:** elias-thorne
- **Public entry seal:** Gold Cactus
- **Character gender:** man
- **Title:** The Town Undertaker
- **Faction:** Dustfall · Undertaking
- **Portrait:** /assets/characters/elias-thorne.webp
- **Accent color:** #B8A1F5

**Public introduction.** Elias measures coffins, repairs musical boxes and remembers every name in the cemetery. He has come to the Eclipse to discuss dignified funeral customs with visitors who may live for centuries.

**Personality.** Soft-spoken, exacting and unexpectedly tender; his darkest observations arrive politely.

**Costume.** A long black coat, silver spectacles, violet flower and a small notebook tied with ribbon.

**Private background.** You inherited the undertaking shop with its debt, its tools and its meticulous records. Graves wanted a cheap pauper’s cemetery; you insisted every person deserved a name. You came tonight to obtain a written guarantee that alien visitors would receive the same dignity.

**Personal secret.** You have been helping indebted families move away by issuing false burial certificates. Six people in your cemetery book are alive and free in another territory. Exposure would ruin your business and return those families to railroad debt.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: you left the dining room at 8:52 to straighten a memorial wreath in the quiet coat passage. You were alone with the flowers for several minutes and returned after hearing movement near the rear gallery.

**Objectives**

- Secure a public commitment that every casualty, human or alien, will receive a proper record and funeral.
- Keep the false burial certificates out of official hands, or win someone’s promise to protect the families named in them.

**Relationships**

- **Dr. Beatrice Bell:** Beatrice has quietly directed desperate families to you. You respect her courage and disagree only about how much danger a good deed justifies.
- **Sera Lumen:** Sera asked how Earth remembers the dead. Your conversations have become an unlikely friendship built on careful names and very old songs.

**Roleplay invitation.** Remain courteous even when saying something chilling. Carry a notebook, offer people a seat, and never let your profession become an excuse to announce unearned forensic facts.

## Reverend Gabriel Flint

- **Character ID:** gabriel-flint
- **Public entry seal:** Violet Cactus
- **Character gender:** man
- **Title:** The Frontier Preacher
- **Faction:** Dustfall · Chapel
- **Portrait:** /assets/characters/gabriel-flint.webp
- **Accent color:** #F3D780

**Public introduction.** Gabriel turned a freight shed into a chapel that welcomes strangers from any horizon. He will bless the treaty tonight, provided somebody can agree which way an alien congregation should face.

**Personality.** Warm, theatrical and stubborn about second chances; he listens before delivering a sermon.

**Costume.** A midnight coat, ivory clerical collar, gold sun pin and a dog-eared book with blank pages.

**Private background.** Before you found Dustfall, you survived by giving people convincing stories about a better future. The chapel began as shelter for a stranded rail crew and grew into a calling. You hope the treaty proves that a community can change its habits as well as its fortunes.

**Personal secret.** You once performed confidence tricks under the name Gideon Glass. A poster in Graves’s private collection shows your younger face. Your present work is sincere, but you fear the town will mistake your changed life for another performance.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: you practiced the treaty blessing in the upstairs prayer alcove from about 8:51. You went downstairs after the blackout, but the alcove has a second stair and nobody checked when you left it.

**Objectives**

- Get two people from different factions to make a practical promise of mutual aid after tonight.
- Find a safe way to acknowledge your former identity before someone else uses it to destroy the chapel.

**Relationships**

- **Ada Crowe:** Ada let you shelter hungry travelers in her storeroom before the chapel existed. You owe her more than any sermon can repay.
- **Ambassador Orion Vex:** Orion has asked whether a blessing can include the sky. You enjoy his curiosity and worry that your public reputations rest on equally fragile words.

**Roleplay invitation.** Offer brief parables, then ask a direct question. Your warmth should invite conversation; save the grand sermon for a moment when the room has earned it.

## Augustus Pike

- **Character ID:** augustus-pike
- **Public entry seal:** Mint Comet
- **Character gender:** man
- **Title:** The Railroad Magnate
- **Faction:** Dustfall · Iron Meridian Rail
- **Portrait:** /assets/characters/augustus-pike.webp
- **Accent color:** #D89A64

**Public introduction.** Augustus brought rails to Dustfall and expects the town to remember the expense. He calls the treaty a golden opportunity and has already drafted a speech about tracks reaching all the way to the stars.

**Personality.** Expansive, calculating and allergic to being ignored; every compliment sounds like an offer.

**Costume.** A burgundy waistcoat, gold watch chain, silver sideburns and a rolled-up railway prospectus.

**Private background.** You built the Iron Meridian by making ambitious promises and keeping enough of them to borrow again. Dustfall’s treaty could open markets beyond imagination. You want the rail company seated at that table before its creditors discover how thin the company’s fortunes have become.

**Personal secret.** Your celebrated railroad is nearly insolvent. You have been paying last month’s interest with next month’s wages. The ledger in your waistcoat records a disastrous bond issue; it does not record a purchase of alien technology.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: at 8:50 you withdrew to the billiard annex to revise your speech and inspect a bond certificate. You claim you stayed there until the shot, although the annex door opens onto the rear gallery.

**Objectives**

- Win a public endorsement for a lawful rail-and-starship trade route before the company’s debts become gossip.
- Convince a worker or creditor that you will honor the overdue wages instead of making another grand promise.

**Relationships**

- **Marigold Shaw:** Marigold can keep a train running when your managers cannot. She has earned your respect and is in a position to demand much more than compliments.
- **Florence Rook:** Florence refuses to certify ore on the strength of your handshake. Her honesty is useful to investors and deeply inconvenient to you.

**Roleplay invitation.** Speak as though every disagreement can become a business arrangement. Let panic flicker when someone asks for actual figures, then recover your magnificent composure.

## Tobias Wren

**Public introduction.** Tobias can hear a loose contact in a storm of telegraph clicks. Since the crash, he has been trying to teach a machine from another world to say hello without setting the curtains on fire.

**Personality.** Earnest, curious and prone to explaining his feelings as if they were circuit diagrams.

**Costume.** Round spectacles, rolled teal sleeves, suspenders and a copper headset or telegraph key.

**Private background.** You taught yourself electrical engineering through mail-order diagrams and patient mistakes. Translating Asterian signals has given you a glimpse of a career beyond tapping out other people’s telegrams. Graves offered to fund your studies if the treaty succeeded.

**Personal secret.** You have been sending unsigned poems over the closed commercial wire to an unknown correspondent aboard the ship. The company would dismiss you for stealing bandwidth. You have hidden the romantic exchange among otherwise ordinary relay logs.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: you adjusted a noisy receiver in the telegraph niche shortly before nine. You stepped into the passage when its signal died, and cannot account for the whole blackout from a working clock or witness.

**Objectives**

- Persuade an Asterian guest to explain one peaceful use for the damaged ship’s technology.
- Recover the private poem tape before the investigation turns an embarrassing correspondence into public entertainment.

**Relationships**

- **Captain Lyra Nine:** Lyra has been explaining star charts in exchange for lessons in Morse code. You admire her certainty and fear looking foolish in front of her.
- **Evelyn Sterling:** Evelyn uses your office as a newsroom annex. You enjoy her company but know that a private wire makes irresistible newspaper copy.

**Roleplay invitation.** Ask permission before sketching a theory. When nervous, compare conversations to crossed wires; when someone is kind, let your sincerity replace the technical language.

## Finch Mercer

- **Faction:** Dustfall · The Tables

**Public introduction.** Finch has never lost a card game he will admit to remembering. He arrived for the Eclipse tournament and stayed for the treaty, where the stakes appear considerably higher than a pot of silver dollars.

**Personality.** Elegant, amused and relentlessly observant; he prefers a raised eyebrow to a raised voice.

**Costume.** A violet waistcoat, polished boots, narrow tie and a deck of cards with one conspicuous joker.

**Private background.** You live by reading what people hope the cards will say. Graves asked you to run tonight’s friendly tournament, a generous description for a table surrounded by rivals who distrust one another. You came prepared to win enough money to leave the circuit behind.

**Personal secret.** You marked a deck to recover a family heirloom from a cheat who had first cheated you. That rigged deck is still in your pocket. You cannot expose the original swindle without admitting that your celebrated streak is partly manufactured.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: you counted the tournament chips alone in the little cashier’s booth at 8:54. You say you waited there through the dark; its service hatch also opens into the gallery and nobody watched it.

**Objectives**

- Get your family’s silver signet ring back through negotiation rather than another crooked game.
- Read the room well enough to name one truthful statement and one bluff, explaining the evidence for each.

**Relationships**

- **Delphine Dusk:** Delphine knows the difference between skill and a marked deck. She has kept your professional embarrassments quiet, and expects the same courtesy.
- **Augustus Pike:** Augustus lost generously at your table, then tried to settle with railway bonds. You have begun to suspect that your winnings are mostly decorative paper.

**Roleplay invitation.** Never force another guest to gamble or disclose a secret. Offer playful wagers about public evidence, and allow one honest admission to cost you visible pride.

- **Faction:** Asterian · Delegation
- **Portrait:** /assets/characters/orion-vex.webp
- **Accent color:** #79B8FF

**Public introduction.** Orion speaks for the stranded Asterian delegation with immaculate courtesy and a translator that dislikes frontier slang. He believes tonight can turn an accidental landing into a lasting alliance.

**Personality.** Formal, diplomatic and quietly mischievous; he collects local sayings with alarming precision.

**Costume.** Blue face paint or accessories, a gold-trimmed violet frock coat and a silver swept-back crest.

**Private background.** You were appointed ambassador after the crash because you could apologize in twelve languages. The delegation needs local food and spare materials; the town needs assurance that the ship is a neighbor rather than an invasion. You have staked your career on this agreement.

**Personal secret.** Your diplomatic credentials expired before the ship left home. You acted beyond your authority to prevent a frightened crew from threatening the town. If headquarters learns this first from a hostile report, your peace could be branded a fraud.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: you rehearsed a translation in the curtained reception booth after 8:52. The booth was empty when you entered and the translator lost its clock during the blackout, leaving your departure unrecorded.

**Objectives**

- Secure a spoken commitment that the treaty will be judged on its terms rather than on your personal credentials.
- Ask a human guest to teach you a local courtesy, then use it sincerely in a tense conversation.

**Relationships**

- **Reverend Gabriel Flint:** Gabriel treats your language mistakes with kindness. You sense that he also knows what it means to grow into a title after assuming it.
- **Nyx Ash:** Nyx is charged with protecting you and knows that you sometimes omit awkward facts. She will follow a lawful order; do not confuse that with unquestioning trust.

**Roleplay invitation.** Be ceremonious without being cold. Translate an idiom too literally once, then demonstrate that your attention to people is sharper than your command of slang.

- **Rowan Quill:** Rowan keeps proposing that the ship grow replacement parts. You respect his patience and wish he would keep roots out of your ventilation ducts.

**Roleplay invitation.** Examine imaginary machinery with your eyes rather than touching props or people. Explain one technical point in ordinary words and grudgingly admire a human invention.

**Public introduction.** Rowan finds Earth magnificent, particularly its cacti and alarming habit of putting fruit in pie. His botanical exchange is the treaty’s gentlest promise: food that can thrive in Dustfall’s exhausted soil.

**Personality.** Dreamy, attentive and unexpectedly tenacious; he asks plants for opinions before people.

**Costume.** Sage-green accessories, leaflike temple fronds, an ochre poncho and a sealed specimen vial.

**Private background.** You joined the expedition to study living systems, not negotiate mining rights. Dustfall’s soil could recover if its residents accept your drought-resistant seeds. You hope the treaty will protect an experimental garden instead of making it another piece of somebody’s property.

**Personal secret.** You planted a small test bed on mayoral land without permission. Its night-blooming flowers sing faintly, which the town has mistaken for a haunting. You have been sneaking out to water them and allowing the ghost story to protect the experiment.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: you went into the rear conservatory to check a wilting specimen around 8:51. You say you remained with it until the shot, but the conservatory’s open service door offers no independent proof.

**Objectives**

- Obtain a promise of protected land for a lawful shared garden, with neither faction claiming exclusive ownership.
- Choose whether to explain the singing flowers before frightened guests decide that Dustfall is haunted.

**Relationships**

- **Cassidy Blaze:** Cassidy distrusts untested seeds near her cattle, but she has shown you every spring on her land. You want her to become a partner in the experiment.
- **Keth Solaris:** Keth complains about your seedlings and secretly repairs their lamps. You know his grumbling is one of his forms of affection.

**Roleplay invitation.** Notice little details and invite others to slow down. You may be fanciful about plants, but keep the distinction between a botanical observation and proof of murder clear.

## Ada Crowe

- **Title:** The Eclipse Proprietor
- **Faction:** Dustfall · Eclipse Saloon
- **Portrait:** /assets/characters/ada-crowe.webp

**Public introduction.** Ada built the Eclipse from a leaky gambling room into the one place everyone in Dustfall will enter. Her hospitality hosts the treaty tonight, and she expects humans and aliens alike to mind her furniture.

**Personality.** Commanding, generous and fluent in pointed silence; she notices who pays and who pretends.

**Costume.** A violet velvet jacket, high curls, a ring of gold keys and a polished glass carried like a scepter.

**Private background.** The Eclipse is the first thing you have owned that nobody can order you to leave. You offered it as treaty ground because every faction already owes you a meal or a favor. Graves promised the event would cancel the saloon’s municipal lien.

**Personal secret.** A concealed room beneath your pantry shelters travelers escaping exploitative labor contracts. You have paid fines and lied about deliveries to keep them safe. The room is separate from the ship’s core vault and has no passage to it.

**Prepared alibi.** Your account: you checked a late supper in the pantry at 8:54 and sent the kitchen help out to hear the speeches. You say you stayed behind to tally the plates, leaving a few minutes nobody can corroborate.

**Objectives**

- Keep the sheltered travelers out of the investigation, or win a trustworthy promise of protection before naming them.
- Have the guests agree that the Eclipse remains neutral ground even if tonight’s treaty falls apart.

**Relationships**

- **Reverend Gabriel Flint:** Gabriel has fed the travelers in your hidden room without asking for repayment. You trust his good heart more than his polished stories.
- **Delphine Dusk:** Delphine’s performances bring custom and chaos. You protect her backstage privacy; she owes you a replacement chandelier and a straight answer about it.

**Roleplay invitation.** Host the conversation as though it were your saloon: welcome shy guests, interrupt cruelty and ask useful questions while polishing an imaginary glass.

## Evelyn Sterling
