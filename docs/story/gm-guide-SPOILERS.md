# Blood Moon over Dustfall — Game Master Guide

**SPOILERS — the murderer, method, motive and question answers are described below. Keep this guide away from guests and the shared display.**

## The case you are running

Dustfall, Halloween 1887. Six weeks after an Asterian ship crashed nearby, the town and its visitors meet at the Eclipse Saloon to sign a peace treaty. Mayor Solomon Graves dies during a blackout. A theatrical shot creates the wrong first impression; the Asterian power core is stolen, and an automatic containment field traps everyone inside town.

The engine chooses one attending character as culprit. There are twenty fully supported culprit variants, one for each playable role. The actual crime always stays the same: the culprit had arranged to auction the living core to an off-world buyer under the alias **Black Star**. Graves’s final treaty clause would put the core under joint custody and forbid private sale. The culprit killed him before signing to preserve the window for theft and profit.

At 8:56 the culprit lowered the main breaker. At 8:57 they carried the public demonstration induction coil into the office and applied it to Graves’s brass peace medallion, causing the lethal electrical strike. At 8:58 a blank from a theatrical revolver staged a gun murder. At 8:59 the culprit removed the core from its cradle. The cradle triggered the containment field at 9:00, spoiling the buyer’s collection plan. The core stays inside Dustfall until the theatrical recovery after the reveal.

The random culprit is not a random answer. Each public character profile has a unique entry seal combining one color with one symbol. Independent traces in the gallery and core/escrow records identify the color and symbol attached to the same continuous live signature. Players must intersect those features against the public seal mapping to identify the guest, then connect the route and sale to the method and motive. Pre-results evidence, assigned clues and searches never print the culprit’s name or an identifier that encodes it. Only results and the private culprit confession provide the full named reconstruction. Never change the culprit after starting or suggest that the trusted records are forgeries.

## Before guests arrive

Use one computer or television for the public stage. Keep the master controller on your own phone; it is a separate seat from the four to twenty suspects. Have each player join with a distinct name and keep the phone they used to join. Reconnecting restores that player’s private character and actions.

The cast has ten men and ten women. Each guest can request man, woman or either. Explicit preferences are respected. Flexible preferences, including legacy nonbinary preferences, can match either character gender; a character’s gender is not a requirement about the player’s identity. Reserve named characters before starting if desired. A reservation must match an explicit preference, and a full gender pool requires changing a reservation, changing a guest’s voluntarily chosen preference or reducing that pool. Do not promise eleven characters of one gender.

The default is three acts of five minutes each. Add time for introductions, discussion and the final reveal; most groups should allow about 35–50 minutes. The master advances acts manually, even if a timer expires. Pause when needed. A one-act game presents the final story evidence; a two-act game presents the opening and final story acts. Both independent seal features and the public mapping remain available, so shorter settings still require and support the same deduction. Optional acts four and five review the timeline and corroborate the seal features without naming the answer before the vote.

Public party-kit signs and character badges are in `public-party-kit.md`. All twenty complete dossiers are in `character-bible.md`, which is also spoiler material. The browser-safe cast in `shared/characters.ts` contains only public introductions and appearance cues. The dossiers, solutions and question answers belong in the server-only story module.

Entry seals are public facts, not extra secrets. The four colors are Mint, Coral, Gold and Violet; the five symbols are Cactus, Comet, Spur, Sun and Eye. All twenty combinations are unique. Keep the complete public list available in a four-player game as well as a twenty-player game. A decorative costume color or a character’s ordinary portrait accent is not their seal color; use the labeled entry seal.

If a saved room predates the Dustfall cast, the upgraded game returns it to a fresh lobby while preserving seats and character-gender preferences. The notice explains that the master can deal the new cast. Recheck reservations and start a new deal; do not try to continue an old archetype mystery with the new evidence.

## Read this opening aloud

“Dustfall has survived drought, cattle feuds and the day the sky fell into the desert. Tonight, beneath the blood moon, we attempt something stranger: peace. Mayor Solomon Graves and our visitors from the stars will sign their agreement here at the Eclipse Saloon. Check your grudges at the door if you can. Keep your stories close if you cannot.”

Let guests read their private phones, introduce their public names and choose ready. They may introduce a costume, mannerism or concern from the public profile. They need not expose personal secrets.

When advancing to the first investigation act, read the displayed narration. It introduces the death and stolen core; do not improvise a different cause of death, a second victim or an unseen attacker.

## The private characters and culprit overlay

Every guest receives a full personal story: background, separate secret, prepared alibi, two objectives, two relationships and a roleplay tip. The separate secrets give innocent characters reasons to be evasive without changing the core case. They include forged documents, protected patients, labor organizing, a forbidden garden, professional embarrassment and disputed authority. None is itself proof of murder.

The culprit receives an additional crime confession. It overrides the proposed blackout alibi for the crime interval; the base secret still happened and can still be played. That guest may lie about their own conduct, distract with their unrelated secret or argue against an inference. They may not rewrite the app’s facts, claim to be a second culprit or demand that an essential clue disappear. If a guest is uncomfortable lying, they may simply decline to explain their whereabouts and let the evidence do the work.

Relationships are optional social prompts. If the related character is absent, a player can mention the history without inventing a substitute player. Objectives are conversation goals, not app-scored tasks, and no relationship, profession or secret is needed to unlock the solution. Do not award unannounced points for aggressive acting or successful deception.

## Act I — A Shot in the Eclipse

Read the public evidence. The medallion and chest burn establish a direct electrical strike. The missing induction coil was portable, had a universal control and was demonstrated publicly. Blank wadding and the absence of a bullet wound establish that the gunshot was theater. The death clock stops at 8:57; the shot occurs at 8:58.

The field is a response to the core leaving its cradle. It is not a murder weapon. The first mention of the battery-lit audit camera seeds the later recovery: the camera was already recording, its tape is jammed rather than destroyed, and its independent light works during the blackout.

Invite each guest to share one observation or a timed account. Anyone can search once, share their assigned clue and answer the case question. Publishing an earlier assigned clue remains useful in a later act; repeated sharing does not earn repeated points.

**Case answer:** An induction coil killed Graves; a blank shot then staged a gun murder. Explain that the sound everyone remembers came after the electrical strike. Do not name the culprit in this act.

## Act II — The Black Star Bargain

The 8:47 escrow offer predates the murder and advertises a living Asterian core. Payment depends on an intact core and an unsigned treaty. Graves’s imminent custody clause would prevent a private sale, establishing the commercial motive and urgency.

The connected rear gallery makes one-person access possible. Every admitted guest could traverse it, and every guest had been shown the coil’s universal switch. The escrow’s damaged seal has not yet yielded the independent features needed to identify its owner. Do not steer the group toward an engineer, an alien, a gun owner or whoever disclosed the darkest unrelated secret.

**Case answer:** Someone planned to sell the stolen core and needed Graves’s treaty to remain unsigned. The alias establishes a plan, not an identity. The proposed treaty creates joint custody and forbids private sale; it does not grant ownership to Graves.

## Act III — What the Lantern Kept

The recovered gallery material yields the entry-seal color associated with the breaker and fatal coil interval. Independent core-removal and Black Star escrow records yield the seal’s symbol. The records attach these separate features to the same continuous live signature; live-contact verification excludes a borrowed badge. No record supplies a face, a name or a character-encoded seal number. Ask the guests to find which public seal belongs to both the color group and the symbol group, then connect that deduction to the route, timing and sale.

Both essential seal features appear automatically on the shared stage. The complete twenty-character public seal mapping is available on profiles and printable badges, including roles not attending. No search choice, clue publication, trivia answer or absent role can block the solution. The unique intersection must be interpreted by the players; the stage does not announce its owner. Searches and varied assigned slips add corroboration and context rather than repeating a complete accusation on every phone.

Let the group test the evidence. Ask for an objection and an answer. A color alone matches five public seals; a symbol alone matches four. Their intersection is unique among all twenty. The records are trusted case facts; the culprit can contest an interpretation but cannot declare the independent traces and live-contact record all fake.

**Case answer:** Intersect the independent entry-seal color and symbol traces, then connect that public seal to the route and core/escrow records. A role’s embarrassing personal secret or technical occupation does not override the linked observations.

## Optional Act IV — The Minute Between

This corroboration act keeps the same culprit. Compare the independent clocks: 8:56 blackout, 8:57 strike, 8:58 staged shot, 8:59 theft. An off-town escrow copy corroborates the seal evidence without printing its owner’s name. The full route takes under three minutes and needs no secret tunnel or second attacker.

Ask a small group to reconstruct the minute-by-minute route while another gives the strongest evidence-based objection. Keep the independent color and symbol traces and the public seal mapping visible. Let the players state their deduction.

**Case answer:** The sequence shows that the shot was staged after the lethal electrical strike. Hearing the shot does not prove where its listener was during the previous minute.

## Optional Act V — Before the Last Bell

The buyer withdraws because the containment field remains closed. The core is still inside town. Invite concise closing arguments that explain the seal intersection and distinguish the linked records from the social secrets. This act offers corroboration and discussion time while preserving the unnamed puzzle until the vote.

**Case answer:** Identify the person whose public seal matches both independent features, then explain method, motive and timing. No confession is required. Popular agreement alone is not proof.

## Scoring and final accusations

Each player can earn 25 for their one search per act, 25 for publishing that act’s assigned clue once and 100 for a correct case answer. A suspicion vote during investigation is changeable and anonymous in the aggregate display. It is a discussion aid, not the sealed final vote, and clears each act.

At final accusations, each guest votes once for another player. Votes remain sealed until results and cannot be changed. The master sees submission progress. Let everyone finish; if you intentionally override missing votes, explain that you are proceeding without them. A correct individual accusation earns 200. A uniquely highest accusation catches the culprit; a tie or no effective accusation lets the culprit escape and earns that player 200.

Read the app’s selected-name solution. Reveal the timeline, then let the culprit offer a short in-character closing line if they wish. Recover the decorative core only after the app establishes the truth. Invite guests to tell the unrelated secrets they enjoyed concealing and say what became of the treaty.

End with: “The blood moon moves west. The lanterns come up. Dustfall has one more story it will never tell the same way twice.”

## Helping a stalled room

- If everyone argues about guns, point back to the lack of a bullet wound and the blank wadding.
- If a profession becomes an automatic accusation, remind guests that the public demonstration and shared route gave everyone opportunity.
- If a private secret dominates, ask which observed part of the core-theft chain it explains.
- If the group misses the commercial motive, read the custody clause beside the buyer’s payment terms.
- If the seal deduction is overlooked, ask guests to list all seals matching the recovered color, then all seals matching the recovered symbol. Which public profile appears on both lists? Let them name the intersection.
- If someone invents an unseen conspirator, ask which available observation requires that extra person.
- If only a few players talk, give each guest one uninterrupted observation before reopening discussion.
- If the game is paused or a phone disconnects, wait for reconnection or the master’s deliberate continuation. Do not request another player’s private screen as a workaround.

## Authoring guardrails for future changes

Keep the induction-coil method, staged blank and Black Star auction motive synchronized across every act, search, private slip, confession and result. Do not give a base role a proven continuous alibi or an assertion of innocence. Keep absent characters out of the required proof chain. Preserve the unique public entry-seal mapping: stable deck index selects Mint, Coral, Gold or Violet by index modulo four, and Cactus, Comet, Spur, Sun or Eye by the integer part of index divided by four. Every culprit variant must receive independent public color and symbol evidence. No pre-results case text may print the culprit’s name, full named reconstruction or a character ID disguised as a seal. Correct question answers and explanations belong only on the server until their scheduled resolution.
