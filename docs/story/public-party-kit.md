# Blood Moon over Dustfall — Public Party Kit

**Spoiler safe. Print or share this document with every guest.**

## Invitation

Dustfall, Halloween 1887. The saloon doors swing open beneath a red moon. Cowboys, inventors, gamblers and visitors from a fallen star have gathered to make peace at the Eclipse Saloon. Dress for the frontier, the stars, or a little of both. Bring a charged phone and a willingness to ask an awkward question with a straight face.

One separate game master controls the evening from a phone. Four to twenty guests play suspects on their own phones; the computer or television shows the shared stage. Costumes and acting experience are optional.

## Quick rules

1. Join using the room code or QR. Choose a character gender: man, woman or either. There are ten characters of each gender. A chosen gender is respected; the master can adjust reservations before starting.
2. Read your private character dossier on your phone. Keep it private, introduce the public version of your character and mark yourself ready. One guest secretly receives the culprit confession.
3. During each investigation act, read the shared case evidence, talk to the other guests and compare their accounts. Every character has a public entry seal made from one color and one symbol. Compare independent traces with the seals on the public profiles and badges; one feature alone can fit several guests. The stage evidence is available to everyone.
4. Search one location per act on your phone to earn 25 points and receive an extra clue. Publish your assigned clue to earn another 25 points once. Share the displayed evidence faithfully even when your character is bluffing.
5. Answer one case question per act. A correct answer earns 100 points, with the answer explained after the act. Your answer locks when submitted. An optional suspicion vote can be changed during the act; the stage shows aggregate suspicion without naming voters.
6. When the master opens accusations, submit one sealed vote for another player. It cannot be changed. A correct accusation earns 200 points. A culprit who escapes the group verdict earns 200 points. Nobody sees the final tally until the reveal.
7. The master reveals the culprit, the evidence and the party awards. A clear highest accusation catches the culprit; a tie or no effective accusation lets the culprit escape. Points celebrate participation and do not decide which account of the crime is true.

You may bluff about your own motives, feelings and whereabouts. You may choose when to disclose your personal secret. You must not edit, fabricate or conceal required app evidence. No character may invent an extra witness, a new forensic result or a power that cancels the game’s facts. Friendly suspicion belongs to the characters; everyone should get room to speak.

## Signs to print

### Welcome to the Eclipse

**THE ECLIPSE SALOON**

Neutral ground. Every horizon welcome.

Tonight: peace talks, Halloween entertainment and a toast beneath the blood moon.

### Act I — A Shot in the Eclipse

**THE LIGHTS HAVE FAILED. THE MAYOR IS DEAD. THE TOWN IS SEALED.**

What did you observe? What have you only heard?

Start with the shared evidence. Compare the times. Give every guest a voice.

### Act II — The Black Star Bargain

**EVERY PROMISE HAS A PRICE.**

Follow the bargain. Examine the route. Distinguish access from identity.

Ask what the treaty would change, and who hoped to change it first.

### Act III — What the Lantern Kept

**THE DARK DID NOT KEEP EVERY SECRET.**

Compare the independent color and symbol traces with the public entry seals. Test an alibi. Build an argument the whole room can inspect.

Before accusing, name the observation that would change your mind.

### Final Accusations

**ONE NAME. ONE SEALED VOTE.**

Choose another player on your phone. Your vote locks when submitted.

Wait for the shared stage to reveal the truth.

## Simple props and room dressing

- Place a violet cloth, gold paper stars and warm battery lanterns around the shared screen.
- Put a labeled empty box at the entrance as the saloon’s ceremonial weapon check; costumes need no weapons.
- Make a treaty from an aged-paper sheet with blank signature lines. It is atmosphere, not additional evidence.
- Wrap a small battery light in translucent mint paper for an imitation power core. Keep its placement decorative unless the master announces a purely theatrical recovery after the app reveal.
- Use a deck of cards, spool of ribbon, printed telegraph strips and mineral-colored glass as background texture.
- Put character badges where guests can take them after assignment. Nobody should receive a private dossier intended for another player.

## Public character badges

Cut each entry into a badge or place card. Names, entry seals and costume cues are public; the phone holds the private story. Each seal combines one of four colors with one of five symbols. All twenty combinations are unique. Keep the complete public list available even when fewer than twenty characters attend.

### Marshal Silas Vale

**Entry seal: Mint Cactus**

**The Weathered Marshal · man · Dustfall · Law**

Silas kept Dustfall alive through cattle wars and a sky that suddenly fell to earth. Tonight he has checked every weapon at the Eclipse Saloon door and promised that the peace will hold until morning.

**Play it:** Patient, dryly funny and difficult to impress; he treats a promise like a loaded weapon.

**Wear it:** A plum or brown duster, battered hat, silver mustache and a brass star pinned over the heart.

**The Borderland Courier · man · Dustfall · Outriders**

Jed can cross a hundred miles of bad country with a message nobody else will carry. A pardon brought him to the treaty, although the marshal still counts the silverware whenever Jed smiles.

**Play it:** Restless, charming and quick with a joke when a conversation gets too close to home.

**Wear it:** A sand-colored hat, red bandana, worn boots and a messenger satchel full of folded maps.

Elias measures coffins, repairs musical boxes and remembers every name in the cemetery. He has come to the Eclipse to discuss dignified funeral customs with visitors who may live for centuries.

**Play it:** Soft-spoken, exacting and unexpectedly tender; his darkest observations arrive politely.

**Wear it:** A long black coat, silver spectacles, violet flower and a small notebook tied with ribbon.

Gabriel turned a freight shed into a chapel that welcomes strangers from any horizon. He will bless the treaty tonight, provided somebody can agree which way an alien congregation should face.

**Play it:** Warm, theatrical and stubborn about second chances; he listens before delivering a sermon.

**The Railroad Magnate · man · Dustfall · Iron Meridian Rail**

Augustus brought rails to Dustfall and expects the town to remember the expense. He calls the treaty a golden opportunity and has already drafted a speech about tracks reaching all the way to the stars.

**Play it:** Expansive, calculating and allergic to being ignored; every compliment sounds like an offer.

### Tobias Wren

**The Telegraph Prodigy · man · Dustfall · Telegraph Office**

Tobias can hear a loose contact in a storm of telegraph clicks. Since the crash, he has been trying to teach a machine from another world to say hello without setting the curtains on fire.

**Play it:** Earnest, curious and prone to explaining his feelings as if they were circuit diagrams.

**Wear it:** Round spectacles, rolled teal sleeves, suspenders and a copper headset or telegraph key.

### Finch Mercer

**Entry seal: Gold Comet**

**The Velvet Cardsharp · man · Dustfall · The Tables**

Finch has never lost a card game he will admit to remembering. He arrived for the Eclipse tournament and stayed for the treaty, where the stakes appear considerably higher than a pot of silver dollars.

**Play it:** Elegant, amused and relentlessly observant; he prefers a raised eyebrow to a raised voice.

**Wear it:** A violet waistcoat, polished boots, narrow tie and a deck of cards with one conspicuous joker.

### Ambassador Orion Vex

**Entry seal: Violet Comet**

**The Celestial Diplomat · man · Asterian · Delegation**

Orion speaks for the stranded Asterian delegation with immaculate courtesy and a translator that dislikes frontier slang. He believes tonight can turn an accidental landing into a lasting alliance.

**Play it:** Formal, diplomatic and quietly mischievous; he collects local sayings with alarming precision.

**Wear it:** Blue face paint or accessories, a gold-trimmed violet frock coat and a silver swept-back crest.

### Keth Solaris

**The Starship Engineer · man · Asterian · Engineering**

Keth has spent six weeks coaxing a broken starship to breathe again. He attends the treaty with tools still in his pockets and a sincere interest in whatever humans mean by a reliable steam engine.

**Play it:** Blunt, practical and affectionate toward machines; he gives useful advice at inconvenient times.

### Rowan Quill

**Entry seal: Coral Spur**

**The Extraterrestrial Botanist · man · Asterian · Life Sciences**

Rowan finds Earth magnificent, particularly its cacti and alarming habit of putting fruit in pie. His botanical exchange is the treaty’s gentlest promise: food that can thrive in Dustfall’s exhausted soil.

**Play it:** Dreamy, attentive and unexpectedly tenacious; he asks plants for opinions before people.

**Wear it:** Sage-green accessories, leaflike temple fronds, an ochre poncho and a sealed specimen vial.

Ada built the Eclipse from a leaky gambling room into the one place everyone in Dustfall will enter. Her hospitality hosts the treaty tonight, and she expects humans and aliens alike to mind her furniture.

Beatrice has patched ranch hands, rail crews and one very embarrassed Asterian pilot. The treaty may bring her a proper clinic; for now, she is prepared to treat either species with the same steady hands.

Cassidy’s cattle were the first creatures in Dustfall to meet the crashed starship. She came to the treaty to protect the grazing lands and to discover whether alien horses are as sensible as her own.

Florence can tell fool’s gold from a fortune by lamplight. Meteor fragments have made her assay office the busiest room in town, and she has agreed to certify the treaty’s exchange of unusual minerals.

### Nyx Ash

### Sera Lumen

**Entry seal: Violet Eye**

**The Memory Archivist · woman · Asterian · Living Archive**

Sera preserves songs, agreements and the small things civilizations forget to write down. She will record the treaty in a crystal archive, though she suspects the saloon’s piano deserves a record of its own.
